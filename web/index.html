The prompter they can't see

A small window that reads your screen and listens to your call, sitting on top of everything you do — and absent from the stream everyone else is watching.

Download for macOS Read the docs

Free and open source. Your own API key. No account, no server.

Measured, not claimed

Every tool in this category says it is invisible. Apple's own developer forums say the flag they all depend on stopped working, with an Apple engineer stating there is no public API for preventing screen capture.

So it was tested instead of assumed. Three windows — one protected, one excluded by filter, one unprotected control — captured through a live 30 fps SCStream, the same ScreenCaptureKit path Zoom, Meet, Teams and OBS use.

The control is the point. Without a window that should appear, “nothing was captured” proves nothing.

control, unprotected 100% captured in every frame
sharingType = .none 0% hidden in all 151 frames
excludingWindows 0% hidden in all 151 frames
Method and caveats

Sees your screen

One key captures whatever you're looking at and sends it with your question. The overlay is excluded from that capture too, so the model never sees itself.

Hears the call

System audio is transcribed as people speak and dropped straight into the chat box, so answering is one keystroke. Split at pauses, never mid-word.

Answers as you

Add your CV and it answers in the first person from your real history — and is told not to invent employers, dates or numbers that aren't in it.

Your key, your data

Gemini, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Keys are encrypted in the system keychain. Nothing routes through a server of ours, because there isn't one.

macOS, no warnings, one line
brew install --cask akshatgg/tap/souffleur
Installs and opens with nothing to click through. Or take a file below.
macOS Apple Silicon .dmg · M1 and newer · 122 MB macOS Intel .dmg · x64 · 127 MB Windows 10 and 11 .exe installer · 206 MB

macOS: the first dialog is not the end. You will see “Apple could not verify Souffleur”, offering only Done and Move to Bin. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway at the bottom. Once only. Neither build is signed by a certificate the vendor recognises, which requires a paid developer account.

Windows: SmartScreen says it protected your PC — click More info, then Run anyway.

On macOS, then grant Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Without it the overlay runs but cannot see your screen or hear the call.

Audio capture is macOS-only for now. On Windows the overlay, screenshots and chat all work; hearing the call does not.
